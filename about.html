OUR JOURNEY

From Paper Attendance Sheets to a Modern Campus Operating System.

EduPulse was founded in 2024 by educators and school principals who were exhausted by cumbersome, outdated legacy software requiring expensive servers, manual backups, and weeks of staff training.

We set out to build a streamlined, cloud-based platform tailored specifically for modern K-12 academies, private day schools, international institutions, and multi-campus educational districts.
